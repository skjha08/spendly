Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and password. When you use Spendly, we also store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly for you: to sign you in, to store and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you ask for. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on our servers and kept for as long as your account is active. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may contact Google's servers when you visit the site. We do not share your expense data with any third party. If this changes, we will update this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, or want your account and data deleted, please get in touch at privacy@spendly.example.
